# Questions for MILLZ Global Solutions

*Forward this as-is. It is written to be answered by the business owner, not by
a developer.*

---

Hi — the website is built and working. What it needs now is **you**.

Right now the site describes the business accurately but generally. It has no
dates, no places, no names and no faces, because I have not made any up. That is
deliberate: this site is going to be read by a credit union loan officer, and
everything on it has to be something you can back up. But it does mean the site
currently reads like a template, and the fix is real detail from you.

**Three things before you start:**

1. **If something isn't true, or you don't know, just say so.** "Not yet",
   "don't have one", "prefer not to publish" are all perfectly good answers. I
   will leave it out rather than guess. Nothing goes on the site that you cannot
   evidence.
2. **Specific beats polished.** "I train clients in my garage gym in North
   Mankato" is worth more than any amount of marketing language. Don't tidy your
   answers up — write like you'd say it out loud.
3. **Nothing is published without you approving it first.**

Part 1 takes about ten minutes. Part 2 is worth sitting down for. Parts 3 and 4
you can do whenever.

---

## Part 1 — Quick facts (about 10 minutes)

These are mostly copy-paste, and they matter more than anything else on this
list. A company page with a formation date and a file number on it is
independently verifiable — that is exactly why a lender trusts it.

**The company**

1. Exact legal name, as registered with the State of Minnesota. (I currently have
   *MILLZ Global Solutions LLC* — is that character-for-character correct?)
2. What month and year was the LLC formed?
3. What is the Minnesota business file number? (It's on your filing, and it's
   public record.)
4. Who is the registered agent?
5. Is the LLC currently in good standing?
6. Do you trade under any other name (a DBA or assumed name)?

**Contact**

7. What phone number should the website publish? *(Important: the site currently
   shows `(507) 555-0100`, which is a fake placeholder number. It must be
   replaced or removed before launch.)*
8. Is `info@millzglobalsolutions.org` a real inbox you check? Who reads it?
9. What hours do you actually answer the phone or reply to email?
10. Is `millzglobalsolutions.org` the domain you want to use? Do you own it yet?
11. Do you want any mailing address published? *(My recommendation is no if it's
    your home address — the site is currently built without one on purpose.)*

## Part 2 — The parts only you can write (worth half an hour)

### About you

**This is the most important section on the entire website.** When someone is
deciding whether this is a real business, they look for a real person. That
section is currently empty and visibly marked "unfinished".

12. Your full name, exactly as you want it published.
13. What title do you want? (Founder? Owner? Founder and trainer?)
14. **How did you get into training people?** Tell it as a story — where you
    started, what you were doing before.
15. **Why did you start your own company instead of working for a gym?**
16. **Why do a training business and a clothing brand sit under one company?**
    This is the question a lender will actually have. In your own words.
17. What's your own athletic or training background?
18. How long have you been training people?
19. **Certifications:** for each one — the exact name, who issued it, what year,
    and can you send me a photo of the certificate? *(Nothing goes on the site
    without the certificate. If you have none yet, that's fine — we leave the
    section off rather than imply otherwise.)*
20. What do you want someone to know about you before their first session?

### Where and how you train

Right now the site says "Mankato and southern Minnesota". That is the **first**
thing someone wants to know and we don't answer it.

21. **Where do sessions physically happen?** A gym you rent? Your own space? Do
    you go to the client? Outdoors in summer? Be specific — name the place.
22. Do you travel to clients? How far will you go?
23. Which towns should we list? (e.g. North Mankato, St. Peter, Eagle Lake,
    Lake Crystal, Le Sueur — tell me which are real.)
24. What equipment is available where you train?
25. Roughly what's the split between in-person and online clients?
26. **Describe how you actually work with someone new** — what happens in session
    one, and what's different by week eight? This is the thing that separates you
    from a gym membership, and I can't write it for you.
27. Who is *not* a good fit for you? (Being honest here builds more trust than
    claiming you can help everyone.)

### Money and logistics

28. **Please confirm or correct every price.** These are my estimates based on
    Minnesota market rates — none of them came from you:
    - Free consultation — 30 minutes
    - Single session — $60
    - 4-session pack — $220
    - 8-session pack — $400
    - Small group, 2–4 people — $30 per person
    - Online coaching — $175/month
    - Youth athletic development — $50/session
29. How do people pay you? (Cash, card, Venmo, invoice?)
30. Is "24 hours notice to cancel" correct?
31. Do session packs expire?
32. What actually happens in the free consultation?
33. Do you train under-18s? How do you handle parent or guardian consent?
34. Do you carry liability insurance? *(Not published — a lender may ask.)*

## Part 3 — Photos, proof and the apparel brand

### The biggest visual gap

35. **There is not a single photograph of the training business.** Every image I
    have is apparel. The site currently shows a labelled empty box where training
    photos should go, because using a hoodie photo to represent a personal
    training business would misrepresent it.

    Can you get photos of: a session in progress, the space you train in,
    equipment in use? Phone photos are completely fine — real and slightly rough
    beats polished and fake. Landscape orientation if you can.

### The apparel brand

36. Is the online store live? What's the URL? *(The site currently shows
    "placeholder — apparel site URL required" where the link should be.)*
37. Where else do you sell? Events, markets, retail stockists?
38. **Is `@toughclothin` your account?** One of your photos has that TikTok
    watermark on it. What handles do you want linked from the site? *(A live
    social account is one of the strongest signals that a business is actually
    trading — right now nothing is linked.)*
39. Where does the name "Mental Toughness" come from? What does it mean to you?
40. Do you hold your own inventory, or is it print-on-demand?
41. You sent 8 product photos (hoodies, shorts, pants, a shirt). Can I use them
    on the parent site? Do you have smaller web versions, or should I resize
    them? *(They're currently 2–6 MB each, far too big to put on a website.)*

### People in photographs

42. **Two photos show identifiable people wearing your product.** Do you have
    written permission from them to use their images commercially? This needs
    settling before the site goes public — it's a legal question, not a design
    one.
43. Is the store in the customer photo yours, or a stockist?
44. Two photos appear to show someone boxing, and one of them has a name on it.
    Is that you? Can I use those images, and how do you want to be described?

### Proof

45. **Are there any clients who'd be willing to be quoted by name?** Even one
    real quote changes the page more than anything else. The section is already
    built and currently hidden because it's empty. I will not write these.
46. How many clients are you training right now? *(Factual — you decide whether
    we publish it. It's often a strong number even when it feels small.)*
47. Any real press, partnerships, sponsorships or affiliations?

## Part 4 — Programs you're building

The site lists youth mentorship and boxing/self-defense as **in development**,
clearly marked as not running yet. I want to keep that honest but make it
useful.

48. **Youth mentorship** — what stage is it really at? Do you have a target start
    date, an age range, or any partner organisations lined up?
49. **Boxing and self-defense** — what stage? Do you have a venue? Are you
    certified or licensed to instruct it?
50. Is there anything else planned that we should list?
51. For either of these — is there anything you *can* commit to publicly yet
    (a season, a waitlist), or should they stay as "being built"?

## Part 5 — Quick decisions

Just yes/no.

52. Publish how many clients you currently train?
53. Publish social media links? Which accounts?
54. Do you want online booking (a calendar people can book into directly)? It
    converts much better than a contact form.
55. Do you want a Google Business Profile set up? *(Free, and it's the single
    biggest thing for getting found by someone searching "personal trainer
    Mankato".)*
56. Do you want a news or blog section? *(My honest advice: no. An empty or
    out-of-date blog looks worse than not having one.)*
